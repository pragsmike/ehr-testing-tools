## What the census actually turned up

Five things that were not known before this session, each measured
rather than reasoned:

1. **`gate v2` is structurally vacuous over this project's own output.**
   At MSH-12 `"2.3"`, and with `hapi-structures-v24` the only structure
   library on any classpath, all 747 messages resolve to
   `ca.uhn.hl7v2.model.GenericMessage$V23` under the judge's own
   `PipeParser`. No segment order, no cardinality, no primitive typing.
   ADR-0174 section 2(d) had named the version question and correctly
   declined to answer it from memory; this is the answer.

2. **The 2.4 flip is one field away from free.** With MSH-12 set to
   `"2.4"` and nothing else changed, 346 of 747 fail -- and all 346 are
   the same cause, `PID-13` failing HAPI's US-phone primitive rule. With
   PID-13 blanked, 747 of 747 parse into real v2.4 structures. Probed
   directly: `"(303)292-0567"` passes, `"492-292-0567"` does not. The
   persona regex in the contract stays `^\d{3}-\d{3}-\d{4}$`; the fix is
   in `pid-segment` alone.

3. **NK1 has no source, so arc 4 cannot have it.** `person-fold`'s
   `demographic-kinds` excludes the `:household-*` family by name;
   `engine/run` returns `{:ground-truth :state-history :facility
   :providers}` and no `:person-index`. The tempting fallback --
   inferring co-residence from PID-11 -- is unsound by measurement:
   `places.edn` is 24 rows, and in this corpus 86 of 89 MRNs sit on an
   address shared with another MRN, one address carrying 7.

4. **The ladder would be invisible to the oracle.** `witnessed-event-kinds`
   contains neither `:order-placed` nor `:result-available`, and
   `witnessed-message-types` no `ORM^O01`: **no oracle root places an
   order.** A status-ladder sweep would report `IDENTICAL` across all 39
   roots while changing every order in every gated corpus. Recorded in
   the ADR so the sweep cannot cite that IDENTICAL as evidence.

5. **The A08/A31 split is lopsided, and it relocates the arc's point.**
   Only 1 of 193 `:demographic-update` and 1 of 72 `:coverage-change`
   events in the probe corpus carries an `:encounter-id`: demographic
   churn happens almost entirely between encounters, because the person
   process walks 20 years while the clinical content is one shift. The
   event-driven half of chatter is therefore ~99.5% A31. The A08 volume
   -- which is what ADR-0168 named the downstream consumer as wanting --
   comes from the PERIODIC half, not the event-driven one. A sweep that
   shipped only the event-driven half would satisfy the design and miss
   its purpose.

And one constraint that binds every add-on:
`v2-replay/evolve-entry` **throws** on an unhandled trigger, which kills
the emitter-coherence property rather than failing it softly, and makes
the bed board count a silent skip. Every add-on co-lands a fold arm.

## Deviations from the prompt, disclosed rather than absorbed

* **(d) NK1 is designed as a STOP, not as a wire surface.** The prompt
  asked which limitation rows it lifts. The answer is none, and the ADR
  says why with three independent measurements. The routes to a wire
  surface are (i) ground-truth change, which the session's own fence
  calls arc-3 territory, or (ii) a non-log emission input, which would
  put content in a message that the log does not contain -- the
  derivability law itself, not a convention.
* **(B)'s channel lean adjusted in one place.** The prompt's lean was
  "A08/A31/IN1 chatter + NK1 + DFT first". NK1 is dropped from v1 for
  the reason above; the rest of the lean is recommended unchanged.
* **(g) `--sink mllp://host:port`, not a `--transport` flag.** The
  designator vocabulary and its round-trip law already exist (ADR-0017),
  and `ehrt play --sink`'s own help already says `dir:` and `blaze:` are
  recognized-but-deferred. Extending the designator costs no flag and no
  help group.
* **A28 on `:registered` added to the chatter family.** Not in the
  prompt's list; the census found `:registered` is the one silent kind
  with no recorded reason anywhere in the tree, and an A28 is the
  message that silence is hiding.
* **The build-session skill's step 13 is stale on one point.** It asks
  for "one index line in each directory's `README.md`". ADR-0147 moved
  those per-file rows into generated `INDEX.md` files, and
  `index-completeness-test` reads `INDEX.md` for both dated
  directories. `make state-derived` writes them; nothing was added to
  either README by hand.

Author ruling, verbatim, 2026-11-23: "Accept your recommendations. Go." -- to these four and the two fields below them:
1. "What makes an order refillable? I recommend: it is chronic, or it states a positive refill count."
2. "Does a stated count bound the chain? I recommend yes: at most that many refills, and a stated 0 means none even on a chronic order. An order with no count runs on the 0.94 draw alone."
3. "Dense's chronic order ... I recommend stating 11, a year at 30 days. ... Renewal, meaning a new order when refills run out, would be named as owed."
4. "Interval from supply. Where an order states a duration, the next fill comes that many days later plus a drawn offset of -2 to +5 days, as a config option. Orders with no duration keep the 28-to-35-day range."
Fields: "Days-supply: the order's duration in days, where stated. Quantity: dose amount times frequency over that supply, where both are stated. For an inpatient dispense I'd use one day's supply ... labelled provisional."

Channel expectations (not rulings; correct from the tree and report):
- Two optional keys on `:medication-dispense`, named in the tree's style (`:days-supply`, `:quantity`), absent when the order states too little to compute them. `classify-change` should call the bump additive; quote it.
- Days-supply: a duration in weeks is 7 days each; any unit the tree cannot convert exactly leaves the field absent. Quantity = amount x frequency x (supply days / period in days), as an exact number; a period in hours converts exactly; a non-integer result is kept as authored arithmetic gives it, not rounded -- say what type the schema gives it.
- Inpatient: days-supply 1 and one day's doses when the order states a dosage, whatever its duration; nothing when it does not.
- Refillable: `(:chronic order)` or a stated `:refills` greater than 0. Bound: when `:refills` is stated, no fill number above 1 + that count; the continuation draw still applies inside the bound. The two draws stay fixed in number per fill, whether or not the bound then stops the chain.
- Interval: one new facility option for the offset range, default [-2 5], schema-validated; with a stated duration the next fill is queued at supply days plus the drawn offset, never less than one day; without one, the existing `:refill-interval-days`. For a 30-day supply that is the same 28 to 35, so dense's intervals should not move -- measure whether the draw lands identically.
- The order record keeps what the chain needs; a fill carried through a merge reads the same prescription.
- A new invariant: where an order states `:refills`, its fills number at most 1 + that count. The numbering invariant stands.
- dense-7500's chronic order states `:refills 11`. At 28,024, refills fall from 27,638 to roughly 14,000 (the channel's arithmetic: the sum of 0.94^k for k = 1..11 is about 7.8 per filled chronic order, times 1,799). Not measured.
- `osteoporosis-dispense` (alendronate, chronic, no prescription block) is untouched by the bound and by the interval rule; expect it identical.

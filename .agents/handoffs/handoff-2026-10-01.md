  :to 1440} {:type :discharge}]` on `decide :cancel-discharge`'s success
  arm (`churn/reinstated-stay-delay-minutes`). An authored pathway that
  continues a stay after a cancel-discharge now runs after the closer.

## What Worked Well / What To Do Differently

**Worked.** Predict-then-measure for the rebaseline (both scripts
equalled the prediction); measure-first halts on every channel
premise; holding red commits locally until green successors exist;
one record per session with a "For downstream" headline.

**Do differently.** Widen a rebaseline's prediction to every gate,
not only oracle roots and gated corpora. Run `bin/preflight` first,
every time. When a handoff's step has two halves, score each.

## Technical Notes

- **One `:pharmacy` stream per patient**, read by nothing else. That
  is why the key reshuffles no ground truth: delete the dispenses,
  renumber, and the keyless log is left (the erasure law,
  `ehrt.sim-engine.dispense-derivation-test`). It is also why a longer
  chain moves a LATER prescription of the same patient: one fill moved
  at 7,500 and two at 28,024 when the tail went.
- **The wire is not held fixed by the erasure law.** The emitter draws
  once per ground-truth event, so added dispenses move message counts
  by single figures with no pharmacy message rendered (ADR-0189, "What
  the erasure law does not cover").
- **A derived fill is queued, never returned beside the event that
  causes it**, because a batch is stamped off the world before the
  batch. A community fill carries no `:encounter-id`, also when it
  lands inside a later encounter.
- **Through a merge** the step is marked `:order-patient-id`; the
  order and its stay are read off the absorbed record, the event is
  the survivor's, the draws stay on the ordering patient's stream.
- **A change to a root's source in `digest.clj`** makes both oracle
  instruments exit 1 at their soundness check though no digest moves;
  `--declared-digest-change` is then owed for the source line.
- **`poly test brick:X` stops a brick at its first failing
  namespace.** Rerun the brick after each fix.
- **`figures.edn`'s `:asserted` block is rewritten by the exerciser.**
  Edit the numbers to what a run will write; `make integration` fails
  on a dirty tree otherwise. The scenario README carries three
  literals the exerciser re-derives from the config files' own bytes,
  which move with any comment edit above them.

## Theoretical/Conceptual Notes

- **Three clocks.** The arrival clock (days), the module horizon
  (decades) and the `:persons` span (twenty years in dense) are
  different lengths, and a bound stated on one is silent or wrong on
  the others. The tail ruling failed on exactly this.
- **Absence is the record.** An unfilled prescription, an undispensed
  admission order and a chain that stopped all leave no event. The
  consumer docs say so in each place; a consumer that reads absence as
  a defect will find thousands.
- **Derived versus authored.** The derived half obeys an erasure law
  and the authored half cannot. That asymmetry decided "Key only" and
  is why no shipped scenario authors a dispense.
- **Realism was bought with stability.** Each of ADR-0188 to the
  addendum moved bytes; each kept the superseded 28,024 log as a
  labelled artifact. That is the working form of "relaxed across
  revisions, not at one".

## What Worked Well / What To Do Differently

**Worked.** A prediction committed before the edit, then a measured
table beside it; measurement-only sessions before a design ruling
(two this arc); one commit per moved thing, so a pin's re-pin is
legible; the "Downstream line" at the foot of every record; holding
red commits until their green successors exist.

**Do differently.** After any version or catalog bump, rerun
`brick:sim` and `make integration` before trusting the brick you
edited. Keep the host idle through a timed drill. When a default is
ruled, write down the measured number it rests on in the same
sentence.

**Working model** (design channel, 2026-11-22): session prompts are
delivered as files in a drop folder outside the tree and the executor
is told the path; results are read by fresh clone; the author still
relays each session's "what the prompt got wrong" list.

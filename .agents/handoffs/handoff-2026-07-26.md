## Mistakes and Lessons

The evidence-discipline-plus-tripwire pattern falsified premises
from every source it has now met, including two new ones this arc:

- **The prompt-writer's** (DOC-1, Step 4): the ruling named
  `:invalid-operator` in `operators.clj` as the error site to extend
  — a registry-load-time schema check never reachable from a CLI
  invocation. Caught at Step 0, substituted, reported.
- **A prior session's inventory** (DOC-3, Step 0): DOC-1's flag
  inventory missed `--lockfile-path`, misfiled by a docstring among
  injection seams. The spec-coverage test now holds it.
- **The design-on-paper's** (DOC-4, twice): two strips were rewritten
  because running them said so — `diff -r` fails on Synthea's
  timestamped filenames (exactly F-registered `:strip-run-timestamp-
  suffix`); an original-vs-mutant `diff` is 59,561 lines of
  reformatting noise. Run-once verification is not ceremony.
- **The cross-repo note's** (CLI-2, Step A4 — a new source): three
  of ehr-testing-sim's claims about this repo did not survive
  source-checking, most consequentially that `corpus intake` "can
  ingest a sim run['s manifest]" — intake never reads a manifest at
  all. Recorded as `[correction]` entries in ADR-0012; the sim side
  must reconcile.
- **A research doc's and a check's own encoding** (parallel
  sessions): F25 refined "segment delimiter CR" with the measured
  LF-pair message separator; the pack-elide session's substring
  check was structurally guaranteed to false-positive over a pack
  containing its own archived prompt. Both begat §7.

Lesson consolidated as doctrine: **checks carry their invariants**
(§7) — DOC-5 was executed entirely under it, including sensitivity
demonstrations proving each new check able to fail before it was
trusted to pass.

Also worth naming: the CLI-2 probe (Step B0) is the pattern's other
face — a premise *surviving* falsification (`make` really cannot
propagate; `.SHELLSTATUS` measured) is what licensed building
`bin/ehr` on it.

## Technical Notes

- **`cli-spec` is now a cross-repo interface** — sim mirrors its
  shape (ADR-0012 property 4). Reshaping the help data model needs
  a shim or a sim-side coordinated change.
- **Reserved category names**: `:gate-no-verdict` and `:cli-help`
  are globally interpreted by the shell (ADR-0012 `[correction]`);
  a colliding per-group use is the breaking change.
- **`quickstart-fresh` extraction contract**: the script's taught
  lines live between its BEGIN/END markers, one statement per README
  command; README quickstart edits now require the script edited in
  the same change — the fast tier will tell you, per-push.
- **`use-cases.edn`**: `:commands`/`:no-commands` are mutually
  exclusive under an `[:fn]` guard; prose belongs in `:note`
  (renders below the fence), never inside a strip.
- **Exit-3 on demand**: no fixture produces a no-verdict aggregate;
  DOC-5's and CLI-2's evidence runs synthesized one via an
  unknown-CodeSystem Patient (see CLI-2 B0's commit body).
- **NTFS mode bits**: `core.filemode=false` here — new executables
  need `git update-index --chmod=+x` (bin/ehr, bin/quickstart-demo
  both 100755 in the index; verify with `git ls-files -s`).

## Watch-Outs for Successor

- Read **ADR-0012 before refactoring anything near `cli.clj`'s
  parse/dispatch/help/result surfaces** — five properties are now
  cross-repo commitments; the dispatch-site comment points at it.
- **§7 binds prompt-writers**: any verification command you mandate
  states its invariant and both failure modes' meanings; structural
  over substring (archived prompts make broad greps false-positive
  by construction); prove new checks able to fail.
- **History stays put, with one refined precedent**: live working
  agreements (this handoff's included) may be *extended* with dated,
  attributed annotations — DOC-3 did so to the 07-25 handoff's
  golden-check line — but records of what a session shipped are
  never rewritten; deviation records append.
- The three verdict-branching teaching moments (gate on mutants →
  exit 1 by design) are asserted as such in `bin/quickstart-demo`;
  a future edit replacing `expect` with `set -e` would re-break
  what CLI-2/DOC-5 fixed.
- The component-granularity locator edge (`PID-5.1` resolves at its
  field) is **documented, not fixed, by ruling** — the grammar is
  intentionally ahead of the substrate, parked like lower/erase.
  Don't "fix" it casually; it's load-bearing for a future substrate.

## Theoretical/Conceptual Notes

The arc's quiet through-line: teaching surfaces became data with
enforcement. `ehr help` and `docs/cli.md` render one spec;
`operators.md` renders the registry; strips render `use-cases.edn`;
the quickstart renders into a script that asserts itself nightly.
The pattern generalizes: wherever a doc and a behavior can share a
source, sharing it converts drift from a review problem into a red
gate. The remaining unshared surface is SETUP.md — deliberately,
because its validation is human, not mechanical.

## What Worked Well / What To Do Differently

Worked: ratify-or-strike rulings kept author decisions cheap without
stalling sessions; phase-severable prompts (DOC-3, CLI-2) let risky
and safe work fail independently; batched convention sweeps in one
commit made "complete or not at all" checkable; sensitivity demos.
Differently: two record nits (a commit count, a suite count)
survived into the plan before ride-along repairs caught them —
close-out numbers should be measured at the final commit, not
carried from mid-session; and the design channel should have
re-pulled before DOC-4's prompt (the SimHospital arc had moved the
board under it — caught only at verification).
